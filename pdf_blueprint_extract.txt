The architecture is intentionally event-driven. The PMS/domain layer remains the transactional source of truth; n8n
handles orchestration and secondary automation; AI agents operate through controlled tools and approval policies.
The commercial strategy established for the project is revenue-first: validate the offer and acquire paying hotels before
building the full autonomous platform.

30. What Not to Build First
 Full enterprise PMS replacement before validating demand.
 Dozens of integrations before the first repeatable deployment.
 Complex machine-learning revenue optimization.
 Native mobile applications.
 Custom POS or accounting ERP.
 Fully autonomous pricing.
 Autonomous financial actions.
 Every AI agent simultaneously.
The architecture should support the end state while implementation remains deliberately narrow. The commercial wedge
is the fastest route to product-market evidence.

32. Architectural Principles
 
Supabase = source of truth.
 Transactional hotel data must have one authoritative home.
 
Domain services = business logic.
 Keep rules out of UI and automation scripts.
 
Events = nervous system.
 Business changes emit standardized events.
 
n8n = orchestration.
 Automation should not be the transactional core.
 
AI Gateway = intelligence boundary.
 Models, prompts, tools, cost and safety are centrally controlled.
 
Agents = controlled decision systems.
 Agents act through tools, policies and approvals.
 
Next.js = human operating interface.
 Optimize for fast hotel staff workflows.
 
Integrations = adapters.
 External providers must remain replaceable.
 
Analytics = measurement layer.
 Operational data becomes management intelligence.
 
Build commercially, not ceremonially.
 Validate the revenue engine before expanding the engineering surface.
